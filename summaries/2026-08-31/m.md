## <#1374989195109466122> — RL & Post-Training

lukedhlee [announced a milestone](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1374989195109466122/1544820877777051678): focusing on RL for Snowball MoE on agentic datasets (r2egym, SWE-Smith). A [pass@8k probe](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1374989195109466122/1545315846342189190) on SFTed Snowball showed pass@1 13% and pass@8 37% on r2egym, with the model identifying correct bugs but failing on multi-file edits — suggesting room for RL sharpening. The 32k context ceiling was flagged as a challenge for long-horizon agentic tasks. Benjamin Feuer [validated Slime RL](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1374989195109466122/1545555200889192508) on TaskTrove data, showing ~40% improvement over baseline with gains concentrated in early steps. Russell Power shared [curriculum RL experiments](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1374989195109466122/1544482297237995591) (3 reactions) finding strict grade-level curricula unhelpful but adaptive difficulty targeting showing promise. romain [highlighted](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1374989195109466122/1544427613085368411) NVIDIA's JAX inference offloading approach (vLLM for forward pass, JAX for backprop) as a way to avoid rollout/training divergences. dlwh [noted](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1374989195109466122/1544405773151379566) the team hasn't finalized policy on proprietary RL data but wants to commit to an open-source-only checkpoint too. lukedhlee also posted a comprehensive [list of feasible research questions](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1374989195109466122/1544753459348246638) for Snowball's first release.

## <#1368297424086499359> — TaskTrove Data Curation

Benjamin Feuer drove TaskTrove from v4.6 to [v4.13](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1368297424086499359/1546234466203336856) across the week, with 94 sources now in Harbor format. Russell Power helped build an [interactive task browser](https://tasktrove.rjp.io/) and a [competency coverage map](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1368297424086499359/1545147565496999979) of all 1.7M tasks. However, dlwh ran a [40-task audit](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1368297424086499359/1545694628756586587) across 20 sources and found 35% broken/hackable, 40% functional but low-value, and only 25% solid. The [QASPER subset was particularly problematic](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1368297424086499359/1545689065347620955) — 49.5% missing the source paper entirely and 74% being duplicates. v4.13 addressed these with 5,426 confirmed-invalid tasks removed and improved verification across 1.7M+ tasks. Michael Ryan shared [IFM TxT360-v2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/IFM/TxT360-v2) (~10T tokens synthetic data), though dlwh was [skeptical](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1368297424086499359/1545190602994483270) it would help large models.
