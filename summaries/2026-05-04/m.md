## <#1364827114670657616> — Iris Scheduler Race Condition & Controller Stability

Ahmed M Ahmed [reported](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1364827114670657616/1501615644842393700) a critical race condition where the Iris allocator placed multiple jobs on the same physical workers, causing 700-1000 preemptions on queued jobs (<https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/5470>). Russell Power identified the root cause and landed a fix in [PR #5490](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/5490), also noting that GHCR unavailability was a contributing factor and [planning to switch to internal mirrors](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1364827114670657616/1501668979079512236). Earlier in the week, Russell [restarted the controller](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1364827114670657616/1501001528046260434) to offload stats management from the controller, reducing load. Gonzalo Benegas [filed an issue](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1364827114670657616/1502361596347941096) about using Marin as a library for bolinas-dna (<https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/5564>); Russell noted the unpublished `finelog` dependency and [welcomed more issue filings](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1364827114670657616/1502369452182999192). dlwh also pinged [PR #5279](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/5279) which would eliminate reservations.

## <#1365044508546568372> — MoE Recipe: MuonH Breakthrough & 1e23 Run Progress

Kaiyue-Wen [achieved a working MuonH configuration](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1365044508546568372/1502824423936491550) for MoE, yielding a 20-30% step-wise speedup over AdamH with no regression on tokens/second — the key was using MuonH everywhere except the LM head. However, Larry [discovered](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1365044508546568372/1502491222105915472) that while Muon holds its ~30% gain on in-distribution FineWeb data through the full recipe, it performs terribly on OOD evals like code (github/cpp: Muon 2.55 vs AdamH 2.2). The 1e23 MoE run is [58% complete](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1365044508546568372/1502435412059226172), with the loss curve tracking the LR schedule as expected. dlwh shared an [aggregate progress figure](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1365044508546568372/1501715305876033559) showing substantial MoE recipe improvements over the past month. Pranshu Chaturvedi's earlier optimizer ablations showed split gate/up plus warmup was [mostly neutral](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1365044508546568372/1500740069026431097). Xa9aX shared the [Aurora blog post](https://tilderesearch.com/blog/aurora) from Tilde Research; Larry [noted](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1365044508546568372/1503123156897108049) it targets wide MLPs which don't typically apply to MoEs.

## <#1462895580064911522> — Data Mixing: IRT Factor Optimization Validated

willheld trained side-by-side MoE models on IRT factor-optimized mix vs proportional mix across [four scaling configs](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1462895580064911522/1501633314770129108) (2.19e17 to 2.83e19).
