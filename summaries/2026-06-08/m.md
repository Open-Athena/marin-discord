## <#1356490712199462912> — Scaling Laws & Delphi

Ahmed M Ahmed kicked off an extensive Q&A on fitting Delphi scaling ladders. willheld [recommended](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1356490712199462912/1513554231594848428) Eric Czech's VPNLS approach (covered in an [OpenAthena blog post](https://openathena.ai/blog/problems-with-chinchilla-approach-2/)) for better fit characterization, and [cautioned](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1356490712199462912/1513554893158223944) that beta1/beta2 are highly data-dependent — though for single-source DCLM with proper shuffling, reusing Delphi's values should be okay. willheld also noted that at 3e18 FLOPs, the model size range is artificially constrained by minimum hidden dim and warmup length.

On LR schedules, Ahmed asked about using linear decay instead of WSD. Larry [shared his mental model](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1356490712199462912/1513596973851086961) that decaying to zero hurts post-training: if you plan to fine-tune afterward, you may want to decay only to something like 0.5× max LR. Ahmed also flagged that the reference hyperparameter sweep code uses linear scales for everything including Adam epsilon — willheld confirmed spending more FLOPs on reference tuning is almost always worthwhile.

## <#1382240679765217342> — Optimizers: Shampoo vs Muon

A lively discussion broke out about the relationship between Shampoo and Muon. Kaiyue-Wen [explained the key difference](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1382240679765217342/1513677658213453954): Muon computes `(EMA(G)EMA(G)^T)^{-1/2} EMA(G)` while one-sided Shampoo computes `(EMA(GG^T))^{-1/2} EMA(G)` — the ordering of EMA and outer product is swapped. He also noted Rohan is mysteriously using one-sided rather than two-sided Shampoo. Ahmed called it ["Newton and Leibniz for optimizers"](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1382240679765217342/1513878777325682799), and dlwh's quip ["There is a little bit of schmidhuber in all of us"](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1382240679765217342/1513699019250077728) was the second-most reacted message of the week.
